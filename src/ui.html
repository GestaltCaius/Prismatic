Nexus cookie: checking

Downloads from Nexus Mods need your nexusmods_session cookie. Open nexusmods.com signed in, then in the browser developer tools go to Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox), Cookies, and copy its value. It is kept in this browser and sent only to this local program. Treat it like a password.
